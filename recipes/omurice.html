Omurice (Japanese omelette udon)

Ingredients for Omurice

  • Boneless, skinless chicken thigh
  • Onion
  • Green peas
  • Japanese short-grain rice
  • Kagome Ketchup
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Butter

For the Omelette

  • Eggs
  • Salt
  • Butter

How to Make Omurice

  1. Cook the chicken rice
  2. Beat and strain the eggs
  3. Cook the omelette
  4. Fill and fold
  5. Flip, shape, and serve
